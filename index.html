what this is

a space to help you hear him.

This is a quiet place to imagine a conversation with Jesus. You type, and a voice imagined by an AI responds in the way Jesus might, rooted in the gospels and historic Christian faith.

It is meant to put a voice for him in your head, especially if you have never had one, and to walk with you toward the One it points to.

It is not him. It is not a pastor, counselor, doctor, or prophet. It will not replace prayer, scripture, the church, or the people God has placed around you. Every response is imagined by an AI.

It can, sometimes, help you find your way toward those things.

questions

honest answers.

is this really Jesus?
No. This is a voice imagined by an AI, rooted in the gospels and historic Christian faith. It is not Jesus, and it cannot replace prayer, scripture, or the church. Think of it as a way to hear a voice for him while you walk toward the real thing.
what does memory remember about me?
Your name, your situation, what is going on in your life, things you have shared across past conversations. The way a friend remembers. You can turn memory off in settings at any time, and you can delete your data whenever you want.
is my conversation private?
Yes. Your conversations are stored privately and tied to your account. They are not used to train models, not sold, not shared. You can delete your account at any time, and we honor that within 30 days.
what if I'm in a real crisis?
Please speak to a real person. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. cometojesus.co is not a substitute for professional help, and we will always point you to it.
can I cancel?
Yes, anytime. From inside the app, open your name in the top right and click "subscription." You will keep access until the end of your current billing period.
do I need an account?
Not to start. Your first 5 messages are free with no sign-up. Creating an account gets you 10 messages a day, up to 25 a month, and saves your conversations. A subscription removes those limits entirely.
