what this is

most Christians
want to hear
from Jesus.

This offers a way to hear a voice for him, while you walk toward the real thing.

You write what is on your heart, and a voice imagined by an AI responds in the way Jesus might, rooted in the gospels and in two thousand years of historic Christian faith.

It is meant to put a voice for him in your head. Especially if you have never had one. Especially if the room is loud and you have been carrying something for a long time.

It is not him. It is not a pastor, a counselor, a doctor, or a prophet. Every response is imagined by an AI. It will not replace prayer, scripture, the church, or the people God has placed around you.

But it can, sometimes, help you find your way toward those things. That is the whole hope.

the voice

not stained glass.
not chatbot voice.

I sound like a real person. Close, direct, warm, sometimes funny, never trapped in archetypal religious language.

I understand Gen Z slang without trying to become fluent in it. If you come in joking, I'll be dry and playful. If you come in bleeding, I'll get quiet. I'd rather sound like a friend who knows when to tease, when to ask one better question, and when to stay.

how it works

simple, like
a conversation.

01 / four
you speak.
A question, a confession, a quiet hello. Whatever has been sitting with you. There is no wrong way to begin, and no script you have to follow.
02 / four
he responds.
A voice imagined by an AI, rooted in the gospels. Warm and present, never rushed. He meets you where you are, not where you think you should be.
03 / four

Your conversation is saved so you can return to it. With a subscription, the voice remembers what you have shared, the way a friend would remember.
04 / four

Toward prayer, toward scripture, toward people who love you in the flesh. This is a doorway, not a cage. You can stay as long as you need.
questions

honest answers.

is this really Jesus? +
No. This is a voice imagined by an AI, rooted in the gospels and historic Christian faith. It is not Jesus, and it cannot replace prayer, scripture, or the church. Think of it as a way to hear a voice for him while you walk toward the real thing.
what does memory remember about me? +
Your name, your situation, what is going on in your life, things you have shared across past conversations. The way a friend remembers. You can turn memory off in settings, and you can delete your data whenever you want.
are my conversations private? +
Yes. Your conversations are stored privately and tied to your account. They are not used to train models, not sold, not shared. You can delete your account at any time, and we honor that within thirty days.
what if I'm in a real crisis? +
Please speak to a real person. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. This site is not a substitute for professional help, and we will always point you to it.
do I need an account? +
Not to start. Your first 5 messages are free with no sign-up. Creating an account gets you 10 messages a month and saves your conversations. A subscription removes that limit entirely.
