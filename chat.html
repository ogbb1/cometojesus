A quiet place to imagine a conversation with Jesus. You type; a voice imagined by an AI responds in the way Jesus might, rooted in the gospels and historic Christian faith.
It is not him. It is not a pastor, counselor, doctor, or prophet. It cannot replace real prayer, real scripture, or real people. It can, sometimes, help you find your way toward those things.
You speak. He responds. Conversations stay in your sidebar so you can return to them.
Without an account, you have 5 messages to start. Sign in for free and you'll have 10 messages a month. A subscription removes that limit entirely.
For paid users, he can remember what you've shared across conversations: your name, your situation, what's going on in your life. You can turn this off in settings whenever you want.
